ABOUT THE PROJECT

House Price Predictor

Linux and Container Technologies capstone for SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur (21CSE216P).

Features: Flask, scikit-learn Linear Regression, PostgreSQL history, Podman multi-container deployment, named volume persistence, and automated tests. Predictions are educational estimates, not financial advice or property valuations.

Dataset documentation, cleaning rules, limitations, Podman commands, and registry-publish steps are in the README.
